# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Griller's Pride customers shop for kosher meat, prepared foods, and fulfillment
options across desktop and mobile. Peter and authorized staff use the same
commerce platform plus authenticated staff tools to review catalog content,
enter and support orders, manage fulfillment, and reconcile operational state.
They often work quickly, in bright office or production-floor conditions, and
need the interface to make the next safe action obvious.

## Product Purpose

The product brings customer shopping, staff-assisted ordering, merchandising,
checkout, fulfillment, payment, and accounting handoff into one observable
system. Success means customers see accurate, customer-safe product facts and
staff can complete high-consequence workflows without hidden state, ambiguous
actions, or losing the audit trail.

## Brand Personality

Expert, trustworthy, and warm. The experience should feel like a capable
family butcher with disciplined operations: direct and human, never clinical,
gimmicky, or generic.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards that prioritize decoration over the work.
- Hidden hover-only controls or unfamiliar interactions that require training
  to discover.
- Dense accounting language, QuickBooks identifiers, or internal product names
  exposed in customer-facing surfaces.
- Over-styled admin tools that make warnings, decisions, and ownership harder
  to scan.

## Design Principles

1. Make operational state and the next safe action explicit.
2. Preserve provenance and history whenever staff revise a decision.
3. Use familiar controls and plain business language so the tool disappears
   into the task.
4. Keep dense workflows scannable through hierarchy, consistent semantics, and
   restrained color.
5. Separate customer-safe commerce truth from internal accounting identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. All staff and customer workflows must work by keyboard,
retain visible focus, use text and iconography in addition to color for state,
support reduced motion, and preserve practical touch targets on mobile.
